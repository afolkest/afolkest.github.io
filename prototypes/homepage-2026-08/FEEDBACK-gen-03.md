## Distilled directives (continue numbering)

14. **No smooth gradients.** Surrounds, mats, and sheets are flat colors
    carrying real, *perceptible* material texture (paper tooth, linen weave,
    laid paper). A gradient may only survive if buried under visible texture
    and no longer readable as a gradient. Note: gen-3's 4.5%-opacity noise
    overlay was imperceptible — that does NOT count as texture.
15. **Artwork frames must behave like real frames.** Uniform border width on
    all four sides, classic finishes — gold, silver, black lacquer — not
    mid-brown bands, not white (standing), not random unequal margins. Slim
    proportions (13's) were right.
16. **No high-contrast fillet/keylines in or around frames.** Any interior
    line sits close to the frame's own value or is absent.
17. **Surround-to-sheet value contrast must be LOW.** 11 (~11 L*) and
    14 (~15 L*) were acceptable; 12/15/16 were "massive contrast" — rejected.
    Target ≈ 10–15 L*. The frame reads through material and line, not value.
18. **No rule/underline under the name.**
19. **15's slate-blue mat is rejected** — cool blue surrounds are out. Stay in
    the hero artwork's warm register (cream / sand / peach / clay, oxblood as
    accent).
20. **Close value alone is not enough** (14 was "ok but boring"). Character
    must come from texture, material, and one or two precise color/ornament
    moves — never from contrast or gradients.
