About

I did my PhD at MIT on classical and quantum gravity — among other things, on when spacetime is allowed to tear apart. These days I build AI research tools at the startup Undermind. Outside work I paint with electromagnetic fields, produce electronic music with choirs and pipe organs, and write Mechanics of Aesthetics.
