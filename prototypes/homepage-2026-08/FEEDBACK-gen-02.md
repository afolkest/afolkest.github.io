## Verdicts

- **06-artwall — KEEP, revised.** "Honestly looks pretty good." But the label
  text should be headlines above each block instead of hanging on the side, so
  the page width can be reduced.
- **07-tallanchor — revised.** Dislikes "All papers" sitting on the headline
  line — it should be a final line at the end of each list. Hates that Music
  has a subtitle on the headline line while nowhere else does — push it back
  down to the individual song lines.
- **08-monograph2 — revised.** Remove the role line ("theoretical physicist /
  computational artist / and research engineer"). This also settles the open
  word-bank question: the meta-description text is NOT fair game as visible
  words.
- **09-framed — lukewarm.** "Not bad. Just feels a bit too much like a
  resume." The format is already resume-adjacent; the frame + margin labels
  push it too far.
- **10-synthesis — interesting idea**, but the side titles printed on the mat
  "feel somehow a bit messy here."
- **General:** spacing between sections was a bit too large across the board.

## Distilled directives for all future generations

8. **"All X →" links never sit on a section's headline line.** They close the
   list as its final line.
9. **Glosses/subtitles attach to individual item lines, never to a heading.**
   No shared subtitle beside or under a section headline.
10. **No role line under the name.** Visible words must come from the
    prototype's visible text — mining the meta description does not count.
11. **Between-section spacing stays moderate.** ~3.2rem at 17.5px body was the
    correction applied; big 4.5rem+ holes between sections read as too airy.
12. **Headline labels above lists beat hanging side labels when the side
    column costs width** (06). Titles printed outside the sheet on the mat
    read as messy (10).
13. **Don't push further into resume territory.** The content is inherently
    CV-adjacent; the design should pull away from that feel, not amplify it.
