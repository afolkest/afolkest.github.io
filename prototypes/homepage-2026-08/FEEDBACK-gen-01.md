## Distilled directives for all future generations

1. **No flimsy/weak type. Recurring complaint — treat as near-invariant.**
   Body and list text must be substantial: Heliotrope grade 4 Medium (400) at
   ~17px+, dark ink. Do NOT set body/labels in grade 3 Book, and do not shrink
   primary text into small letterspaced caps. Headlines/name: strong (Semibold
   600), real sizes — never tiny, never flimsy.
2. **No tiny small-caps section labels as primary navigation.** Labels must be
   substantial (the baseline's 1.35rem gray labels were fine).
3. **Scannability is king.** Clear vertical scan paths; papers and essays must
   be first-class, instantly visible single-line lists — never buried below
   the fold of attention or reduced to captions.
4. **At most 1–2 artworks displayed** (three full pieces is too much).
   Art may be balanced against text but must not crowd it.
5. **The framed-sheet device is liked**: content on a light sheet against a
   different-colored surround/mat. Worth exploring variations (frame color,
   depth, proportions). Interior stays light-background (dark bg + white text
   remains banned).
6. **No pointless horizontal rules.** A rule must earn its place; default to
   whitespace and alignment for separation.
7. **No giant letter-spacing in the name.** The name is set solid, tight,
   bold — like the baseline's h1.
